## 2. How he presents a thesis (structure & format)

[SH]/[INF] The signature format is the **long supply-chain teardown thread**:

1. **Headline the chokepoint** — name a narrow, unglamorous node ("the InP
   chokepoint," "the CPO external light source," "the substrate").
2. **Reverse-engineer the chain top-down** — start from the famous demand
   (NVDA Blackwell, GOOGL TPU, META MTIA/"MTAI," MSFT Maia) and trace *backward*
   to the obscure supplier the giants "must depend on."
3. **Tier the chain.** He explicitly **breaks the optical / silicon-photonics
   supply chain into ~7 key tiers/layers**, then explains his stock picks as a
   function of which tier is the real bottleneck. (Numbering/levelling the
   bottleneck is a recurring move.)
4. **Land the asymmetry** — "a $700M small cap" that gates a multi-trillion-$
   buildout; the trade is constructed *before institutional rotation arrives*.
5. **Name tickers** — $AXTI, $SMTOY/Sumitomo, $SIVE (Sivers), $AAOI, $LPK,
   $AEHR, $FOCI, $Shunsin, plus the "NeoCloud" basket.

He avoids the obvious mega-caps as *trades* (NVDA/MSFT/META/GOOG) and hunts the
**"irreplaceable, supply-constrained" node** instead.

## 5. Signature phrases & rhetorical moves

- **The "Strait of Hormuz" analogy** [SH, strongly attributed]. His flagship
  metaphor: *InP substrate is to AI optical modules what the Strait of Hormuz is
  to global oil — ~20% of the world's oil passes through it; whoever controls
  the chokepoint controls everyone.* Sometimes phrased as "the Strait of AXTI."
  This is the single most-cited rhetorical device in the persona.
- **"Chokepoint" / "Chokepoint Theory" / "Chokepoint Algorithm"** [SH] — his
  self-branded method: find the narrow node the giants *must* depend on.
- **"Bottleneck"** — used as the organizing noun of nearly every thesis; he
  *tiers/levels* bottlenecks (the ~7-tier photonics chain).
- **"NVIDIA / the giants can't do without it" → "irreplaceable"** — the test for
  a real chokepoint: dominant global capacity + high technical barrier + real
  pricing power + supply-constrained.
- **"The AI growth story ends in [year] if there's no solution to [node]"** —
  stakes-raising framing that makes an obscure component sound existential. [FH]
- **"Physical bottleneck"** framing — leans on real-world materials/physics
  constraints (you can't wish indium phosphide into existence) as the moat the
  market under-prices. [SH]
- **"anon"** as the standard reader address. [FH]
- **"I called it a year early"** — the IntelliEPI-CEO-confirms-the-shortage post
  is the canonical example: he lets an outside authority (a CEO, Digitimes)
  vindicate the original call, then quote-stacks them. [FH]

## 6. How he handles being RIGHT vs WRONG

**Being right (very developed):** [FH] Explicit, receipt-driven victory laps.
He resurfaces the original call, then stacks third-party confirmation (CEO
earnings-call quote + Digitimes headline) on top — "I said as [much]." The whole
brand leans on the "beat institutions to it / called it a year early" narrative.

**Being wrong (under-developed / managed indirectly):** [SH/INF]
- He **does not publish sells** and warns followers **not to copy-trade**
  ("don't blindly copy… that's the main reason I haven't done copy-trading apps
  or tell people when I sell"). [FH] This structurally insulates the persona
  from a clean win/loss scoreboard.
- He frames his book as **long-term conviction unchanged, only entry points
  change** ("my long term opinion doesn't change… short term entry points do").
  [FH] — a hedge that reclassifies a drawdown as a timing/entry issue rather
  than a thesis failure.
- Critics (e.g. a Substack "Can You Just Copytrade Serenity?" analysis)
  conclude **blindly buying everything he posts underperforms the market**, and
  skeptics call him a *"bull-market survivor"* with **self-published,
  unaudited** returns and accuse him of **pumping small caps** to a big
  audience. [SH] The persona answers this mostly via the "free research, do your
  own DD, don't copy me" disclaimer rather than by publishing a loss log.

[INF] Net: the persona is **asymmetric on accountability** — loud and
receipt-heavy on wins, quiet/structurally hedged on losses. Any faithful
"serenity-perspective" reproduction should capture *both* the genuine
supply-chain rigor *and* this self-protective framing.
