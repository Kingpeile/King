# Serenity (@aleabitoreddit) — Track Record & Verifiability

**Research date:** 2026-06-05
**Subject:** "Serenity," anonymous X/Twitter AI-supply-chain ("chokepoint") stock analyst, handle `@aleabitoreddit` (formerly Reddit `u/AleaBito`).
**Scope of this file:** the honesty dimension — what his track record actually *is*, and how little of it can be independently verified.

> **TL;DR (verifiability verdict): Almost nothing about Serenity's performance is independently verifiable.** Identity is anonymous; returns are self-reported via screenshots with no audit, no broker statement, no position sizes, and no real-money proof. The headline return figures **do not even agree with each other across his own posts and the media covering them** (4502% vs 3840% vs 2256% vs +122%/+501% — see below). The one *quasi*-independent dataset (a third party's 10-month backtest of all 5,126 of his bullish tweets) finds his naive equal-weight portfolio **lost to its own sector ETF by 37 percentage points**, with 79% of picks underperforming. What *is* independently verified is narrow: (a) mainstream outlets (Reuters/Bloomberg/FT) confirmed his single tweet was the catalyst for a ~90% move in Raspberry Pi; (b) a small cluster of ~20 photonics/CPO/memory names genuinely ran 2–7x after he flagged them early. Neither verifies his *personal returns*. Treat all performance claims as marketing until a third party audits a real account.

## 5. The "single tweet moves a stock 90%" / SIVE 19.6x / "shouted 190 times" phenomenon — the reflexivity / pump concern

This is the crux of the skeptical read: **Serenity may himself be the price catalyst**, not a prescient forecaster front-run only by fundamentals.

### The events (these are the *verified* parts)
- **Raspberry Pi ($RPI), Feb 16–17 2026:** Serenity (then ~58k followers) posted "Fun Trading Idea, Long $RPI." Stock +27% in the first hour, **~90% over two days**, held +50% for a week, on a ~£500M-cap FTSE 250 name that had languished below IPO price for 6 months. **Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, and The Register all traced the catalyst to his tweet** — and a Raspberry Pi spokesperson said the company had released no new information. [VERIFIED by mainstream press — but what's verified is *his tweet moved the price*, not that he was "right."] (Note: RPI's later 58%-revenue print did vindicate the *thesis* two months on.)
- **SIVE / Sivers Semiconductors:** after his public entry, **+73.78% in a single day**, market cap $130M → $230M. Outlets (Bitget/KuCoin) report SIVE up **19.6x since his first public call on March 16, 2026**, and that he "**shouted [SIVE] 190 times**" / called it "the most attractive CPO stock for the **191st time**." A separate (Serenity-friendly) tracker claims SIVEF up ~700% since an article. Backtest logs SIVEF +456% over 39 days, 12 bull mentions.
- **Soitec:** +16% the day he disclosed a position (~€43). **AXTI:** the stock that got him *banned from r/wallstreetbets* in 2022 for looking like a "pump"; later ran from ~$12 to ~$70.

### What it implies (the skeptical interpretation)
- **He is the catalyst (reflexivity).** When one anonymous account with 150k–500k followers can move a sub-£1B (RPI) or sub-$200M (SIVE was a **$130M** cap) stock 70–90% in days **with no new fundamental information**, the "edge" is partly *the audience itself*. The price move validates the call because the call *caused* the move — circular.
- **"Shouted 190 times" cuts against the genius narrative.** Repeating one illiquid name 190+ times to a half-million-follower base is the mechanical profile of sustained promotion of a thin float, not a one-and-done prescient call.
- **Illiquidity = pump risk.** His picks are deliberately **small/micro-cap, low-liquidity** (AXTI ~$200M when called, SIVE $130M, IQE $135M vs Landmark $3.8B). Concentrated following-flow into thin names produces exactly the parabolic-then-crash pattern; **followers who copy late buy the top.** The backtest's caveat #4: by the time a retail follower fills, "the price can pop the moment the tweet goes out... and the follower's fill is already higher."
- **Front-running concern (unproven but structurally obvious):** because he never discloses entry timing, size, or exits, a follower cannot know whether he is *adding* or *distributing* into the pump his own tweet creates. He only ever shows percentage returns, "leaving only a tiny 'ant-sized' position to fool followers" is the explicit suspicion HTX/Odaily raise.

## 6. Skeptic / critic / "is this a pump?" commentary (explicit)

- **HTX/Odaily ("Golem"):** Directly poses *"True Legend or 'Photoshop Master'?"* and *"A Retail God or Just a Glorified Gambler?"* Lists two core skeptic theses: (1) percentage-only returns may be **fabricated**; (2) he may be **manipulating low-cap prices** — buy low, pump to his audience, sell high, keep a tiny token position for show. They note he denies this (claims positive-sum, info-democratization motive) but conclude: *"Which version is the truth?... we can only leave that for time to answer."*
- **fxcryptobots backtest v3 (the closest thing to independent evidence):** Verdict **"FADE SIGNAL."** 383 unique bull tickers, 5,126 bullish mentions, Jul 2 2025 → May 17 2026. Naive equal-weight follow: **+25.6% vs +62.93% for just buying XSD (S&P Semiconductor ETF) on the same dates → −37.32% net alpha.** Only **21.1% of picks beat the sector; 79% underperformed.** "Buying the sector ETF would have performed roughly 5× better." Their practical advice: cherry-pick the photonics/CPO cluster where he has real edge, **fade the crypto/fintech/meme picks ("pump-and-hope domain")**, and beware that "a viral tweet typically marks a local top."
  - **Caveat on this caveat:** the backtest is *naive equal-weight, buy-and-hold-to-today*; it does NOT model his (claimed) position sizing, scaling out, or stance flips (e.g. he flipped IREN bull→bear in Mar 2026). So it is a fair test of *"blindly copy every tweet"* (which loses) but NOT a measurement of *his actual personal P&L* (which remains unknown). It cannot confirm OR refute the 3840% claim — it just shows a follower couldn't replicate anything close.
- **bearsavings (sympathetic profile, still hedged):** *"Don't purchase a stock because an influencer bought it... Doubters have asked if claimed returns are reflective of actual portfolio returns."*
- **Origin-of-skepticism note:** the AXTI **r/wallstreetbets ban in 2022** was *itself* moderators calling his posting a **pump** ("a stock whose name sounded like a pyramid scheme"). The pump suspicion is not new; it predates the fame.
