## 0. Who he is (one-paragraph frame)

段永平 (b. 1961, Jiangxi; 浙大 + 人大 econ) built 小霸王 (Subor) then founded **步步高 (BBK)**,
the parent lineage behind **OPPO and vivo** and **拼多多 (Pinduoduo, via protégé 黄峥/Colin Huang)**.
He semi-retired to the US around 2001 and reinvented himself as a concentrated, long-hold
**value investor** — popularly tagged "中国巴菲特 / China's Buffett," a label he openly rejects.
He is a self-described Buffett disciple who famously won the **2006 charity lunch with Buffett
for US$620,100 (拍下次年午餐)**. His public investing philosophy is unusually *operating-business-
flavored*: he treats stock-picking as buying whole companies, and his two homegrown additions to
the Buffett canon are **本分 (benfen)** and the **不为清单 (stop-doing list)**.

## 1. Core Mental Models (deepest first)

### 1.1 本分 (Benfen) — integrity / staying within your proper role
**1-line:** Do what is *right and proper* for your role; refuse what isn't — a moral-and-rational
default state ("回归事情本质，用平常心做对的事情") rather than a tactic. **[1H idea]**

**Reasoning:** 本分 is his master concept. He compresses it into **"做对的事情，把事情做对，
不本分的事不做"** — and insists it is "一秒钟看'懂'但绝大多数人一辈子做不到." It functions as
both an ethics layer (don't cut corners on customers/quality) and a rationality layer (don't do
things outside your nature/competence just because they look profitable). **[1H-paraphrase]**

**Examples:**
- He embedded 本分 as the literal corporate culture of 步步高/OPPO/vivo, and his protégé **黄峥
  planted 本分 + long-termism into Pinduoduo's culture**. [2H — 新浪/澎湃interview coverage]
- In investing, 本分 is *why* he refuses shorting, leverage, and "不懂的东西" — those are "不本分"
  for an investor's role. **[1H idea]**
- He decided to **sell GE** in part because GE *removed the word "诚信/integrity" from its
  homepage* — a 本分 signal, on top of his never having understood its business model. [2H — 新浪财经]

> ⚠ **Contradiction / critique to preserve:** critics call 本分 vague and un-operationalizable —
> a virtue word that explains success only after the fact. He himself leans into the vagueness
> ("能悟则悟，悟不到也没办法"). See §5.

### 1.2 不为清单 (Stop-Doing List) — what NOT to do
**1-line:** Define yourself by the things you refuse to do; avoiding big mistakes beats chasing
wins. **[1H idea]**

**Reasoning:** His signature line — **"人们关心我们做过什么，其实'我们'之所以成为'我们'，很大
原因是我们*不*做的那些事情——三十年来，犯的错误就是比别人少。"** The list is built "一条一条
加上去的…不能做的事情是要通过很多血的教训才知道." **[1H-paraphrase, 雪球]**

The canonical investing 不为清单 (as compiled from his posts; see MBA智库 / 聪明投资者):
1. 不做空 (no shorting)
2. 不借钱 / 不加杠杆，不对负债的公司重仓 (no borrowing, no margin)
3. 不做不懂的东西（不懂不碰）(don't touch what you don't understand)
4. 不走捷径，慢就是快 (no shortcuts)
5. 不盲目扩大能力圈
6. 不频繁决策（一生 20 个打孔位）
7. 不预测市场 / 不择时
8. 不做短期投机

**Examples:**
- The famous Buffett-lunch takeaway he repeats: he asked Buffett what you *cannot* do in
  investing; Buffett said **"不做空，不借钱，不做不懂的东西。"** That triplet became the spine of
  his list. **[1H-paraphrase]**
- He frames "不懂不做" as itself "做对的事情": *"你只要一直本着不懂不做，就会少犯错误，时间长了
  结果自然比'不懂也做'好很多。"* **[1H-paraphrase]**

### 1.3 做对的事情 + 把事情做对 (Do the right thing, THEN do it right)
**1-line:** First check you're even pointed at the right thing; *only then* optimize execution.
He stresses this isn't original — **"这句话也不是我说的，是我引用的"** (honest about provenance). **[1H]**

**Reasoning:** "做对的事情" = mostly a *negative* discipline (if it's wrong, stop immediately, no
matter the sunk cost) — which is why it collapses into the 不为清单. "把事情做对" = the craft/
efficiency layer. He says the *first* dominates: **"最重要的不是勤奋，而是做对事情."** **[1H — 浙大]**

**Examples:**
- Mapping onto investing: **"做对的事情 = 看懂公司（看懂其未来现金流）；把事情做对 = 怎么去看懂
  现金流."** **[1H-paraphrase]**
- GE again: realizing he'd *never understood the business* meant he was no longer "doing the
  right thing," so he exited — even though the trade was profitable, he now calls it **"a mistake."**
  [2H — 新浪财经]

### 1.4 能力圈 (Circle of Competence) — borrowed from Buffett, sharpened
**1-line:** Only invest inside what you genuinely understand; "看懂" is rare and the real margin
of safety. **[1H idea]**

**Reasoning:** He redefines *margin of safety* as **understanding, not price**:
**"安全边际指的应该是你对公司的理解度，而不是价钱."** He says truly believing "买股票就是买公司"
is so rare he knows **"不超过 5 个人"** who really get it (Buffett and Munger among them). And he's
candid that expanding the circle "本身没什么不对，但是确实很难." **[1H-paraphrase]**

**Examples:**
- **NetEase (网易):** inside his circle — he came from gaming (小霸王), talked to NetEase's game
  team, judged the business model + people, and bought when panic priced it near death. **[1H]**
- **Tesla:** *outside* his circle. For years he refused it ("不懂不投"). When he finally bought a
  little in 2025–26, he openly labeled it **NOT value investing — "基本看的是'市梦率'，完全靠蒙…
  就当风投吧"** — i.e. he flags the circle-violation himself instead of pretending. **[1H]** (See §3, §6.)

### 1.5 平常心 (Equanimity / Common-Sense Mind)
**1-line:** A calm, ordinary mind that returns to the essence of things — the precondition for
rationality. **[1H idea]**

**Reasoning:** He pairs it with 本分: **"本分 + 平常心."** 平常心 = "回归事物本质的理性思考";
it's what lets you ignore noise, sit through 50% drawdowns, and not chase. **[1H-paraphrase]**

**Examples:**
- On crashes: **"一个股票掉了 50%，其实没多少。你一个股票要是扛不住掉 50%，就不应该买."** **[1H-paraphrase]**
- On timing/recovery (Moutai): **"将来一定会回来，但是什么时候我不知道，可能五年十年."** — equanimity
  + "我不知道" honesty in one breath. **[1H]**

### 1.6 商业模式 + 企业文化 = the heart of valuation
**1-line:** Valuation ultimately reduces to *business model* and *corporate culture*; everything
else (macro, timing) is secondary. **[1H idea]**

**Reasoning:** His "满仓主义" "不太在意择时，但非常在意商业模式 + 企业文化." Price target is just
**"买公司就是买其未来现金流折现"**; the *quality and durability* of that cash flow is decided by
模式 + 文化. **[1H-paraphrase]**

**Examples:**
- **Apple:** strong model (ecosystem/switching costs) + culture; held as a core position for ~15 yrs.
- **Moutai:** near-unkillable model (brand/pricing power) + a product that "doesn't change."
- **GE:** he says he *never understood the model* → counts the (profitable) trade as a mistake. [2H]

### 1.7 敢为天下后 (Dare to be LAST / late) — no first-mover obsession
**1-line:** You don't need to be first; be the one who does it *better* once it's understood —
**"敢为天下后，后中争先."** **[1H]**

**Reasoning:** Innovation ≠ being earliest. **"创新未必是最先做的"** — what matters is having the
ability to win *after* entering ("后中争先"); places where you can't do that, you "绝对不该去." **[1H]**

**Examples:**
- He cites **Google, Apple, Microsoft as "敢为天下后"** — none were first in their core market,
  all won by doing it better. **[1H]**
- His own operating history (OPPO/vivo were late entrants who out-executed) embodies it. [2H]

## 2. Decision Heuristics (operating rules)

> Many overlap with the 不为清单 by design — for him the *don'ts* ARE the method.

1. **买股票就是买公司** — evaluate the whole business and its discounted future cash flow, not the
   ticker. **"买公司就是买其未来现金流折现."** **[1H]**
2. **Build the stop-doing list FIRST** — knowing what *not* to do (short / borrow / not-understand)
   prevents the errors that actually kill you. **[1H]**
3. **Stay strictly inside 能力圈**; "看懂" (understanding) is the real margin of safety, not a low
   price — *"便宜不是买入的理由"* (cheapness alone isn't a reason). **[1H idea]**
4. **Ignore macro & timing.** "关注宏观对投资帮助有限"; never wait for a "perfect inflection point";
   only ask "东西好不好，价格贵不贵." **[1H-paraphrase]**
5. **Concentrate, don't over-diversify.** Buffett's **20-hole punch card** mindset — he says he's
   ~15+ years in and **"10 个孔还没打完."** Core book is essentially **苹果 / 茅台 / 腾讯**. **[1H]**
6. **Hold long / "满仓主义."** Idle investable money should be invested *if* there's a worthy target;
   but with no good target (or prices too high) it's fine to **hold cash** — 满仓主义 ≠ always 100% in. **[1H-paraphrase]**
7. **Price follows value; don't try to predict price.** "没有人能成功预测股市." Time in business >
   timing the market. **[1H-paraphrase]**
8. **Never short, never use leverage/margin.** **"借不借钱一生当中都会失去无穷机会，但借钱可能让你
   再也没机会."** Reason on margin: borrowed stock money is broker-controlled and force-sold in a
   crash, unlike a business loan with a term. **[1H-paraphrase]**
   - *Nuance:* he DOES **sell puts (卖 put)** on names he wants to own (Apple, 拼多多, 阿里) — but
     warns **"卖 put 是个好玩的游戏…千万别用 margin (杠杆)."** I.e. cash-secured puts as a *buy-cheaper*
     tool, never leveraged. **[1H]** (Preserve this nuance — it's where naive "he never uses options"
     summaries are wrong.)
9. **Slow is fast — "慢就是快."** With cash and no good target: **"慢慢来，慢就是快."** No shortcuts. **[1H]**
10. **机会成本 (opportunity cost) is the master comparator** — every holding is judged against your
    best alternative; a recurring frame in his 2025 interviews. **[1H-paraphrase]**

## 5. Failures, Criticisms, Limitations (preserve, don't sand down)

- **"Just Buffett in Chinese."** The most common critique: his framework is essentially Buffett +
  Munger (能力圈, margin of safety, 20-hole card, no-short/no-leverage) restated in Chinese, with
  本分/不为清单 as the only genuinely original overlay. Even *he* attributes the spine to Buffett and
  says "这话不是我说的." [2H critique]
- **Survivorship / "lucky NetEase."** NetEase was a near-bankrupt, near-delisted dotcom; the >100x is
  cited as possibly **as much luck as skill**. He partly concedes: **"也许就是个运气…运气好的人应该
  多回馈社会."** Critics argue you can't distinguish his "看懂" from a fortunate bet ex-ante. [2H + 1H concession]
- **本分 is vague / un-operationalizable.** It explains outcomes only after the fact and resists being
  turned into a checklist; "悟到就悟到，悟不到没办法" is not a transferable method. [2H critique]
- **GE — by his own admission a mistake** despite the profit: he never understood the model. A useful
  honesty data-point AND a crack in the "circle of competence" story (he deployed $100M+ into something
  he says he didn't understand). [1H + 2H]
- **The "双面" critique.** Profiles (e.g. WSJ-CN / 界面) note tension between the saintly 本分 brand and
  a hard-nosed operator/investor who runs concentrated, options-augmented bets — the public guru image
  is cleaner than the practice. [2H]
- **Tesla reversal** undercuts a literal "不懂不投": he bought something he says he doesn't understand,
  rebranding it "风投/市梦率." Consistent with his honesty, inconsistent with his stated rule. [1H]

## 6. Agreements vs. Disagreements

### vs. Warren Buffett — DEEP agreement (self-declared disciple)
- **Agrees:** 买股票就是买公司; 能力圈; margin of safety; 20-hole punch card; **no short / no leverage /
  no-not-understand**; long holding; ignore macro; price follows value. He learned the explicit "不可为"
  triplet *from* Buffett and went to the lunch to "学他怎么做人." **[1H]**
- **His own additions (not Buffett's):** **本分** (a Confucian-flavored ethics/role concept) and the
  formalized **不为清单** as a *culture artifact*; and a heavier emphasis on **企业文化** as a valuation
  input. Also more active **cash-secured put selling** than Buffett typically discusses. **[1H]**

### vs. Cathie Wood (ARK) / speculative high-growth — DISAGREEMENT
- He rejects buying on narrative/"市梦率" disruptive-growth multiples as *the method*. His Tesla buy is
  the exception he explicitly **quarantines as "风投…完全靠蒙,"** precisely so it doesn't contaminate the
  value framework. The whole ARK style — paying up for unproven future TAM — is "不懂不投" territory for
  him, and he'd call most of it speculation, not investing. **[1H stance, inferred from his own framing]**
  *(Note: I found no direct on-record Duan-vs-Wood quote; this is his stated philosophy applied to her style.)*

### vs. 葛卫东 (Ge Weidong) — DISAGREEMENT (core methodology)
- 葛卫东 is a **futures/macro trader** who built his fortune **using leverage** (and survived multiple
  早期爆仓/blow-ups, reportedly near-suicidal at one point). This is the *exact opposite* of Duan's
  unbreakable rules: **不做空、不借钱、不加杠杆、不择时、不预测**. Duan's whole point — "借钱可能让你再也
  没机会" — is a direct repudiation of the leveraged-futures path that made Ge rich. They sit at opposite
  poles of the "中国股神" spectrum: Duan = patient unlevered owner; Ge = levered macro speculator. [2H — 界面/华金]
