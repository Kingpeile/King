## 0. SOURCING HONESTY — READ FIRST

**The verifiable corpus on 葛卫东 is THIN, especially compared with Buffett or even 段永平.** Concretely:

- He is **deliberately reclusive**. He has said he refuses to publish market views ("原谅我从来不愿意发表市场观点"), gave very few formal interviews, and largely **退出公众视线 after ~2015**.
- **Almost all of his famous futures "war stories" (大豆/橡胶 10万→20亿, 2010 棉花, 2014 做空铜赚18亿) are SECOND-HAND media/forum retellings** — repeated nearly verbatim across outlets, with no primary trade records, no audited numbers, and no on-the-record confirmation from him. Treat the *numbers* as **legend/rumor**, the *direction/instrument* as plausible.
- **What IS well-verified:** his **A股/美股 equity holdings**, because Chinese listed-company 定期报告 (top-10 流通股东 tables) and IPO 招股说明书 legally disclose them. So 科大讯飞, 兆易创新, 中科曙光, 沐曦股份 etc. positions are HARD, regulator-grade data.
- **First-hand textual corpus** that we DO have: a 2008 和讯期货 interview (his own quotes), and a widely-circulated set of "**29句至深感悟**" — short reflections he reportedly wrote himself (微博/朋友圈 register). These read authentic and consistent, but the exact provenance of each line is not independently auditable, so they are marked **first-hand (self-authored, provenance soft)**.
- **The 上海交大 演讲 / 捐赠 referenced in the task brief could NOT be verified.** Searches of the 上海交通大学教育发展基金会 donor records (May 2026) surface 曾毓群, 陈光明, 季琦, 沈军(其实), 成明和, 米哈游 etc. — **no 葛卫东 donation or named building or campus talk was found.** A university-talk transcript could not be located either. **Do NOT assert the 交大 talk/donation as fact** — flagged as **UNVERIFIED / likely confusion or rumor**. (His real philanthropy footprint is genuinely under-documented in accessible sources.)

So: **thin corpus, high rumor-to-fact ratio on the futures legend, hard data on equities.** Flag accordingly throughout. The "29感悟" is the single richest first-hand window into HOW he thinks.

Marking convention: **[1st]** = his own words (interview / self-authored). **[2nd]** = media/forum. **[REG]** = regulatory/disclosure data (hardest). **[RUMOR]** = unverified legend.

## 2. CORE MENTAL MODELS (deepest 3–5)

### MM1. 顺势 + "倾听 > 预测" (Trade WITH the trend; listen to the market, don't forecast it)
**1-line:** Don't predict the market — *feel/listen* for "阻力最小的方向" (the path of least resistance) and go with it.
**Reasoning:** This is his single most distinctive idea and it's genuinely his own. He fuses (a) classic trend-following with (b) a near-mystical "倾听" stance borrowed from 混沌理论 (chaos theory / 蝴蝶效应) and 太极推手. Markets are chaotic systems → long-term prediction is impossible → so cultivate an *open, empty mind* that detects when a trend strengthens or reverses *first*, rather than committing to a forecast.
**Verbatim [1st]:** "做投资特别是做期货，第一是顺势，第二是用心灵来感受阻力最小的方向。如果你杂念太多的话，你就感受不到哪个方向是阻力最小的了。" (2008 和讯期货 interview)
**Verbatim [1st]:** "对市场有两种态度，一是预测，二是倾听……以我的观点，倾听比预测更重要，更靠谱……所以，原谅我从来不愿意发表市场观点！因为我想要一颗开放的心灵，拥有良好的听力！" (感悟 #11–12)
**Example [REG/2nd]:** His 沐曦/讯飞-era equity behavior shows the *patience* side; the 2014 copper episode [RUMOR] shows the *reversal-detection* side — he flipped from long to short within half an hour when the macro fact (IMF-style growth downgrade) hit.

### MM2. 混沌理论 → 认知谦卑 (Chaos theory → epistemic humility / "认识局限性")
**1-line:** A tiny data error → wildly different outcome, so the market cannot be predicted; certainty is a fantasy, only *probabilities* exist.
**Reasoning:** He explicitly credits Edward Lorenz's chaos theory / butterfly effect as the framework that "changed a Chinese man's investment philosophy." This is WHY he names the firm 混沌 (Chaos). It's the intellectual root of his anti-prediction, pro-risk-control stance.
**Verbatim [1st]:** "一个微小的数据误差会带来与原来截然不同的结果，类似道理，股市同样不可能被长期预测。所以，我也不对股市预测。" (2008)
**Verbatim [1st]:** "最佳的方法一是承认认知的局限性，二是又要确认单个因果关系的存在，然后品味市场的强弱，倾听市场的声音。" (感悟 #14)

### MM3. 风控先于投资 / "投资的真谛是不要赔钱" (Risk control is survival; don't blow up)
**1-line:** Survive first, profit second — control total drawdown and you've controlled risk.
**Reasoning:** For a leveraged futures trader, ruin is permanent. He converts Buffett's "rule #1" into a trader's discipline. Notably he **caps his own futures leverage at <3x** — derived from reverse-engineering how much leverage Soros used breaking the pound, and from noting Soros & Buffett both averaged <30%/yr → stable compounding beats heroics.
**Verbatim [1st]:** "投资的真谛是不要赔钱。挣钱是不难的，难的是二三十年可以长时间不赔钱。" / "只要把亏损的总额控制住，就把风险控制住了，不要激动，过了就过了。" **[1st, via 陆家嘴金融网 '风控先于投资' — note: that page was 403/paywalled; quote corroborated across multiple 2nd-hand outlets, treat as 1st-leaning.]**
**Verbatim [1st]:** "将自己做期货投资的杠杆比例控制在3倍以下……稳定的复利增长，就无人可敌……总结出'不冒险、不激进'的指导原则。" (2008)
**CONTRADICTION to preserve:** the *legend* (重仓+杠杆 commodity blitzes) screams aggression; his *stated* doctrine is "不冒险、不激进" and <3x leverage. Both coexist — he is aggressive on *conviction/size at the entry* but religious about *capping total loss*.

### MM4. 反人性 / 战胜自己 (Beat yourself before you beat the market — discipline over emotion)
**1-line:** Retail loses to 贪婪 & 恐惧; the real opponent is your own psychology, so pre-commit to a plan and kill the "杂念."
**Reasoning:** He's obsessed with trading psychology and behavioral economics ("不懂行为经济学的是做不好期货的"). The recurring move: define the target/strategy in advance, then execute without emotional interference — and he warns that *success itself* is the danger ("出错的时候往往都是踌躇满志的时候").
**Verbatim [1st]:** "散户为什么赔钱，主要还是不能克服贪婪和恐惧这两大人性的弱点……投资的关键是要事先明确目标，要按照之前的策略来做。" (2008)
**Verbatim [1st]:** "做期货要先战胜自己，才能战胜市场。" (感悟 #8) / "做期货最大的一个忌讳就是恐慌心理。"

### MM5. 不动则已，一击必中 (Lion patience — wait motionless, then strike once, decisively)
**1-line:** Investing needs a lion's character: endure, wait, do nothing for a long time, then strike with full size at the one right moment.
**Reasoning:** Counter-intuitively for a "trader," his *equity* sizing is built on extreme patience. He admires Julian Robertson's Tiger Fund. The regulator data confirms it: in stocks he holds **2–3+ years**, sitting motionless.
**Verbatim [1st]:** "不动则已，一击必中。" (his stated 交易之道) / "投资也需要有狮子一样的个性，善于忍耐，学会等待。"
**Example [REG]:** He bought 中科曙光/用友网络/西藏药业 in 2017 and held for years; bought 西藏药业, sat until it 大涨数倍 in 2020, then sold; bought 兆易创新 in 2018 and **still holds in 2025** (兆易创新 持仓市值 >36亿 at Q3 2025). The 沐曦 bet: 混沌 entered in **2022**, he personally added 8亿 in Q1 2025 — and 7hcn frames it as "为了这2个月，他等了8年." **[REG + 2nd]**

## 3. DECISION HEURISTICS (operational rules)

1. **三大开仓条件 (his stated "三个原则") [1st, 2008]:** "(1) 是不是能通过冒1元钱的风险赚3元钱 (≥3:1 reward/risk); (2) 基本面分析是不是支持你投资的方向; (3) 技术面是不是也支持你投资的方向 — 基本面能不能和技术面形成同步共振." → He needs **fundamentals + technicals to RESONATE (共振)** before sizing up. Example he gave: "6000点大家看着能涨到8000、10000点，但它就不涨，开始掉头了，这就是基本面和技术面不相符了，这种就不适合交易."
2. **顺势, feel "阻力最小的方向"** — never fight the tape; flip fast when the trend's character changes (MM1).
3. **Cap leverage <3x; control TOTAL loss** — survival rule (MM3). "把亏损的总额控制住."
4. **小波动一定不要去理会 (ignore small moves)** — derived from a 1996–97 regret: he and a friend each held 10x-baggers (四川长虹 / 深发展) for >1yr but he netted only 3x by churning. Lesson: "持得稳，守得牢." [1st, 2008]
5. **有所为有所不为 (selectivity / information triage)** — refuse the "信息灾难"; "只看新闻联播和第一财经"; cultivate 大局观 from a high vantage instead of drowning in reports. [1st]
6. **认错要快 / 矫枉过正是大敌** — but balanced: "交易最怕矫枉过正，从一个极端到另一个极端……必须在心态平和的前提下扪心自问." (感悟 #19) Admit error, but don't over-correct into the opposite extreme.
7. **等待 = 加仓的前提:** in equities, do nothing until conviction + resonance, then strike big once (MM5).

## 6. FAILURES / CRITICISMS / LIMITATIONS

- **Extreme volatility & blow-up risk:** futures leverage cuts both ways. The 2016 螺纹钢 short loss (~5亿/day reported) is the documented counterweight to the win-stories. His style **can lose enormously, fast.**
- **Style is near-IMPOSSIBLE to replicate & DANGEROUS to imitate.** It relies on (a) a chaos-theory "倾听" intuition built over decades, (b) leverage, (c) macro timing, (d) iron psychological discipline. For a retail reader, copying the *legend* (重仓+杠杆+timing) without the *discipline* (<3x leverage, total-loss cap, 反人性) is a path to ruin. **The skill should foreground his risk-control/psychology layer, not his swashbuckling layer.**
- **Survivorship & rumor inflation:** the most cited "facts" (10万→20亿, 棉花几十亿, 铜18亿) are **unverified legends** repeated across low-quality sources. Real-time disclosed equity data is the only audited record, and even there his *rank* and *exact stakes* shift quarterly.
- **Opaque / refuses to articulate method:** "从不愿发表市场观点." Less teachable corpus than value investors who write annual letters. We are reverse-engineering from sparse interviews + aphorisms.
- **The 上海交大 演讲/捐赠 in the brief is UNVERIFIED** (see §0) — do not build content on it.
- **Concentration:** his book is heavily 科技/AI (讯飞, 兆易, 沐曦) — a sector bet that has worked spectacularly in 2023–2025 AI mania but carries obvious single-theme drawdown risk.

## 7. AGREEMENTS vs DISAGREEMENTS with other 股神

### vs Buffett & 段永平 — SHARP METHOD DISAGREEMENT, partial philosophy overlap
- **DISAGREE (method):** Buffett/段永平 *hate* leverage, futures, derivatives, and timing/macro-trading — 段永平's whole creed is "不懂不做," 长期持有好生意, no shorting, no leverage. 葛卫东 **lives on exactly those**: commodity futures, shorting, macro timing, leverage (capped, but real). 段永平 would consider 葛卫东's core game un-investable; Buffett calls derivatives "financial weapons of mass destruction."
- **AGREE (philosophy/psychology):** They converge hard on **risk control ("不要赔钱" ≈ Buffett rule #1), 认知谦卑, 反人性 (greed/fear), 敬畏市场, 认错, 复利 (he explicitly studied Buffett's <30%/yr → stable compounding)**. 葛卫东 even cites Buffett's "one-time-event price drop = opportunity" logic (中国平安/富通). And his *equity* behavior (2–3yr holds, 不动则已) is surprisingly **value-investor-like patience**. So: **agree on temperament & survival, disagree on instruments & method.**

### vs Cathie Wood (ARK) — overlap on conviction, different domain/method
- **AGREE:** big, concentrated, **high-conviction bets on transformative tech** (his AI book — 讯飞, 沐曦, 摩尔线程 — rhymes with Wood's disruptive-innovation thesis), comfort with volatility, "strike big once."
- **DISAGREE:** Wood is a long-only, narrative-driven, fundamentals-of-the-future equity manager who rides drawdowns on thesis conviction; 葛卫东 is a **trend/risk-control trader at root** who cuts losses and caps total loss, and who explicitly **distrusts prediction** ("倾听>预测") where Wood is famously *forecast*-driven (price targets years out). Same conviction temperature, opposite stance on forecasting.

### Closest spiritual kin
- **Soros / Druckenmiller / Julian Robertson (Tiger)** — reflexivity-adjacent, macro, trend, leverage-aware, "lion patience." He explicitly studied Soros (reverse-engineered his pound leverage) and admires Robertson's Tiger Fund. He is **far closer to the global-macro / trend tradition than to the value tradition** — that is the single most important framing for the skill.

## 8. ONE-PARAGRAPH SYNTHESIS (for skill authoring)

葛卫东 is the **global-macro / trend-following pole** of the 股神 pantheon — the anti-Buffett in method, the fellow-traveler in temperament. His thinking rests on **chaos theory → you cannot predict, you can only LISTEN** (顺势 + "倾听>预测" + 阻力最小的方向), wrapped in fierce **risk control as survival** (不要赔钱, leverage <3x, cap total loss), **anti-human-nature discipline** (战胜自己 before战胜市场, kill greed/fear), and a surprisingly patient **"不动则已，一击必中"** sizing style that, in equities, looks almost like value investing (multi-year holds of 讯飞/兆易/沐曦). His voice is bravado-meets-修行: blunt trader war-talk fused with 王阳明心学 and 太极. **Author the skill around his discipline/psychology/risk-control layer (replicable, wise) and treat his leverage/timing legend as inspiration-not-instruction (dangerous, unverified).**
